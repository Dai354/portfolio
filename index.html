Ultrasonic Parking Sensor

Arduino C++ Embedded Systems Sensors

A car-style parking aid built on an Arduino UNO with an HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor. A 16x2 LCD shows the live distance in inches, while green, yellow, and red LEDs and a buzzer mark Safe, Caution (5–12 in, beeping faster as objects get closer), and Stop (under 5 in) zones. The firmware median-filters readings, adds hysteresis to prevent flicker at zone edges, corrects the speed of sound for air temperature, and logs CSV data over serial.

Rocket 2025 Internship Game

Python Pygame Game Development Pixel Art Piskel

An endless side-scrolling platformer I programmed in Python with Pygame during my 2025 Rocket internship. I designed three of the four playable characters, the falling star hazards, and a rock monster obstacle as pixel art in Piskel. Players jump across moving platforms while dodging falling rocks, stars, and trap blocks, collecting hearts to heal and trophies for temporary immunity.

Platforms are procedurally generated ahead of the player with gaps limited to what a jump can clear, and the game gets harder as the score climbs. It also includes a scrolling parallax camera, landing-only collision, characters you can switch mid-run, a saved high score, and an Inverted Mode where the whole world spins upside down between 1,500 and 3,000 points.

A menu-driven MATLAB program built for BE 1500 that loads real-world datasets on child illiteracy by country. It computes descriptive statistics, sorts and charts data by year, plots country trends, and uses linear regression to predict illiteracy rates for the next ten years.
